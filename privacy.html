Privacy Policy for Soccer Agent Dynasty

Last updated: October 2026.

Soccer Agent Dynasty ("the app") is a single-player football management game.

What we collect

No account, no personal profile. The app has no login and does not transmit your game progress to us. Your career save is stored only on your device.

The app does use one third party for ads, Google AdMob, described below. Apart from that, the app does not collect or transmit your data.

Local storage

Your career save is kept locally using on-device storage. Deleting the app removes it. There is no cloud sync.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may access a device advertising identifier (IDFA), limited device data, and how you interact with its ads (views and taps, which Google also uses to measure ad performance). Google's ad SDK may also send Google crash diagnostics from the app. Before the first ad request, iOS asks through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt whether the app may track you. Where the law requires it, Google's ad privacy form also asks about personalized ads; you can reopen that form any time from Store → Ad privacy choices. If you decline either, you may still see non-personalized ads. No ad is ever required to progress: rewarded ads are entirely optional.

AdMob's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

In-app purchases

This version of the app has no in-app purchases, and it does not contact any purchase service. If purchases are added in a future version, this section will be updated first.

Children

The app is not directed to children and is not part of Apple's Kids Category. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
