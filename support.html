Support — Soccer Agent Dynasty

Soccer Agent Dynasty is a single-player football-agent career game for iPhone.

Getting help

Email memorrowiv@gmail.com. Please include your iOS version, your iPhone model, and what you were doing when the problem happened — that is usually enough to reproduce it.

Common questions

Where is my career saved? On your device only. There is no account and no cloud sync, so a career does not follow you to a new phone, and deleting the app deletes its save.

I bought Remove Ads and it isn't applied. Open the Store screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with. The entitlement is validated against the App Store, so it restores on any device on that account.

Do I have to watch ads? No ad is ever required to progress. Rewarded ads are entirely optional, and the interstitials shown between sessions of play can be removed permanently with a one-time purchase.

Can I turn off tracking? Yes. Decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or change your ad privacy choices any time from Store → Ad privacy choices. You may still see non-personalized ads.

Are the clubs and players real? No. Countries are real; every club, league, competition, player and staff member is fictional.
